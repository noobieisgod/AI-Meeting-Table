# Google Play Privacy Checklist for AI Meeting Table

## Privacy Policy URL

Before submitting to Google Play, confirm that the published privacy policy URL:

- Opens in a normal browser without login
- Is not a PDF
- Is not geofenced
- Shows the app name `AI Meeting Table`
- Shows the developer name `noobieisgod`
- Shows the contact email `minghorn.tmp@gmail.com`
- Does not include analytics, ads, tracking scripts, cookies, or external fonts

## Data Safety Consistency

Make the Google Play Data Safety answers consistent with the privacy policy.

Disclose that the app may process or transmit:

- Prompts, goals, and user messages
- Conversation and session content
- Generated content
- Attachments or attachment content when the user adds files and runs provider features
- API-provider interactions with OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic

Disclose that the app stores locally:

- Transcripts
- Artifacts
- Logs
- Settings
- Attachments
- Selected providers and models
- Meeting table and session state
- API keys in Android secure storage where available

Do not claim:

- All data stays on device
- No third parties are involved
- Analytics, ads, crash reporting, or tracking are used
- A developer-operated backend exists

## Third-Party Provider Links

The privacy policy links to:

- OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Gemini API Terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
- Anthropic Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

## Local Data Deletion Notes

The policy states that users can delete local data by:

- Deleting tables or session content in the app where those controls are available
- Removing attachments where available
- Clearing the app's data in Android system settings
- Uninstalling the app

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private local sessions, transcripts, artifacts, logs, attachments, settings, and locally stored API keys.

## Final Submission Check

- Verify the GitHub Pages URL after publishing.
- Paste the URL into Google Play Console.
- Confirm the Data Safety form does not contradict the policy.
- Confirm the app listing, privacy policy, and in-app behavior all describe the same data practices.
